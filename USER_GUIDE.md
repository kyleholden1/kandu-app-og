# Kandu - User Guide

Welcome to Kandu, your parenting support companion. Here's what each section does.

## The Nest (Home)

Your daily hub for regulation and practice.

### Daily Practice
- New practice prompt appears each day
- Click "Start Practice" to complete it
- Once done, the button grays out until tomorrow
- Tracks your consistency

### How's It Going?
- Use the slider to rate your regulation level
- 😤 Struggling (left) → 😌 Thriving (right)
- Click "Submit Check-in" to save
- Your check-ins build your streak

---

## SOS Mode

Immediate support when challenges happen.

### Quick Guides
- Tap any struggle emoji (Hitting, Tantrums, Biting, etc.)
- Modal opens with a strategy to try
- Read the steps and the "why" behind it

### Strategy Feedback
- **It Worked!** → App remembers this strategy works for you
- **Didn't Work** → App will suggest a different approach next time

### The Adaptation Engine
Kandu learns what works for your child:
- First time you SOS for "Hitting"? Get the first strategy
- Rate it poorly? Next time, get a different approach
- Rate it well? Kandu sticks with what works

## The Stream

Learn & relax between challenges.

### Rest & Restore
- **The 5-Min Reset** (300 sec)
- **Deep Breathing** (180 sec)
- **Walking Meditation** (600 sec)
- **Sleep Prep** (900 sec)

How to use:
1. Click a card to "play"
2. Audio/video simulation plays (card shows pause icon)
3. Auto-completes when duration finishes
4. Check mark appears
5. Progress tracker updates (X/4)

### The Science
Quick reads on parenting neuroscience:
- **Why Toddlers Hit** — Impulse control, not malice
- **The Power of 'Yet'** — Growth mindset changes brains
- **Mirror Neurons** — Your calm creates their calm

Click to mark as read. Builds your learning streak.

## Profile

Your progress & achievements.

### Level
Increases with every 5 check-ins you complete. Reflects your engagement.

### Weekly Streak
Shows the last 7 days. Green checkmark = you did a practice or check-in that day.
- Streak resets at midnight
- Only counts days with activity
- Builds motivation to return

## Data & Privacy

### What Kandu Saves
- Your daily practices & check-in ratings
- Which SOS strategies you've tried & how you rated them
- Which Stream content you've completed
- Your streak

### Where It's Stored
All data lives on **your phone/device** in browser storage. Nothing is sent to servers (for now).

### Access Your Data
- Open browser DevTools (F12 or Cmd+Option+I)
- Go to Application tab → Local Storage
- Look for keys like `kandu_user_data`, `kandu_stream_data`
- This is your data. You own it.

### Clearing Data
If you want to start fresh:
- DevTools → Application → Local Storage → Delete entries
- Or: Settings → Clear Browsing Data → Cookies and Site Data

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of Kandu

1. **Daily Habit**: Do your practice & check-in every morning to build momentum
2. **SOS Honestly**: Rate strategies truthfully—it helps Kandu learn your child
3. **Stream on Hard Days**: Take 5 minutes to reset or learn something new
4. **Watch Your Streak**: Seeing consecutive days builds motivation to keep going
5. **Read the "Why"**: Understanding the science behind strategies makes them stick

## Troubleshooting

**Q: My data disappeared!**
A: Check if you cleared browser data or went incognito. Data is stored in your browser, not on servers.

**Q: A strategy button says "Coming Soon"**
A: All 8 struggles now have full strategies. If you see this, refresh the page.

**Q: The Stream won't mark content as done**
A: Make sure JavaScript is enabled. Try refreshing the page.

**Q: I want my data on another device**
A: Coming in the next update! For now, data is device-specific.
